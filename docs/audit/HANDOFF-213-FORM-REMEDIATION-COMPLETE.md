## Risks / Caveats

1. **Render PASS does not certify legal correctness.** Render fidelity (binding, text, literal, structure) confirms that placeholders resolve to values and layout is preserved. It does not confirm that the right semantic field is bound to the right DOCX slot — that judgment requires domain/legal review.

2. **Generated gate outputs will become dirty after regeneration.** The circular `DECISION_GATE_HEAD_DIFFERS` warning is inherent to the current design: committing gate outputs updates HEAD, making the committed gate stale. Do not keep committing this loop.
